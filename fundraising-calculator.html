How this is calculated

Every number on this page comes from these formulas. Nothing else is assumed.

  • Current runway = cash in bank / monthly net burn
  • Raise needed = (average monthly burn x target months x (1 + buffer)) - cash in bank, never below zero
  • Runway after the raise = (cash in bank + raise) / average monthly burn
  • Investor ownership = raise / (pre-money valuation + raise)
  • Founder ownership after = (1 - investor ownership) x (1 - option pool)

This is a planning estimate, not financial advice. It models flat average burn, not revenue growth, and it cannot tell you what valuation you will get. Use it to set the ask, then test the plan with your accountant or lawyer before you sign anything.
