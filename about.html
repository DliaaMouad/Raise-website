ABOUT RAISE · STRUCTURE BEFORE OUTREACH

Good companies lose rounds when the story fails to land.

The belief behind Raise is simple: strong startups often struggle to raise, not because the business is weak, but because the investment story fails to land. Raise exists to create a fundraising system that gives those companies a fair shot, built on diagnosis, narrative, and a structured process instead of luck.

The problem: a prettier deck, the same fuzzy case

Founders respond by polishing the deck: better design, tighter slides, prettier charts, while the case for investing stays fuzzy and the polish hides it. The meetings end the same way, in polite hesitation: a bit early for us, let us stay in touch.

The hard truth sits underneath. Investors do not read decks to find reasons to invest. They scan them to find reasons to say no. A deck that only looks better gives them nothing new to say yes to, and the founder walks away raising money for a design problem they never had.

The Raise approach

Raise works in the opposite order to a design service. Diagnose the story first, strengthen the investment case, then execute a structured raise aimed at the right investors.

01

Diagnose

We audit your deck, narrative, and unit economics against 10 investor-readiness dimensions and find the fatal leaks before partners do.

02

Sharpen

We rebuild the narrative and positioning until a partner can retell your case in one sentence, and every metric claim holds under fire.

03

Execute

We target the investors who fit your stage, sector, and round size, rehearse the hardest questions, and run the raise as a sequenced process.

Clarity over complexity

If an investor cannot explain your moat in one sentence, they pass. We make your story impossible to misunderstand.

Structure over improvisation

Every slide earns its place. We cut clutter and put the exact proof a partner needs in front of them.

Evidence over exaggeration

Claims need receipts. We build every deck on verifiable numbers, never on hype.

Precision over volume

More outreach cannot compensate for weak positioning. We target the investors who fit, then go to market.

Conviction over desperation

You work directly with Mouad. No juniors, no templates, no handoffs, and no begging for meetings.

Meet Mouad Dliaa

Mouad built Raise after sitting through back-to-back partner meetings pitching what he thought was an obvious winner. The product was great. The traction was real. Yet every conversation ended in polite hesitation.

After dissecting 200+ decks alongside active venture partners, the pattern became obvious: investors scan decks to find reasons to say no. Raise was built to fix that, and to challenge founders early rather than let partners reject them late.

Every Raise engagement is done directly with Mouad. He reviews the deck, the narrative, and the numbers personally, and he is the person on your advisory sessions and your walkthrough calls. No juniors, no templates, no handoffs.

If you are building alone, this is who Raise was built for: a partner-level second brain for your raise, working directly with you from the first diagnostic to the signed term sheet.
