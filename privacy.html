Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Raise (raiseos.site) is a fundraising advisory brand and software suite for pre-seed and seed founders, run by Mouad Dliaa. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account data. When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, country, company name, fundraising stage, and your LinkedIn profile link if you provide one. On the Tools landing page you can also provide a Twitter handle.
  • Tool data. The data you enter into the Raise tools (for example your investor pipeline, outreach drafts, readiness answers, financial inputs, diligence checklist, and simulation answers). If you connect Google Sheets, that data is stored in a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive. Otherwise it is stored in your own browser.
  • Login session. When you are signed in, a session is stored in your browser so you stay logged in across tools and tabs until you sign out.

How accounts work

Accounts run on Supabase Auth. Your password is handled by Supabase and is never stored by Raise in plain text. Your profile (name, country, company, stage, LinkedIn, Twitter) is stored in a Supabase database table. Supabase acts as our authentication and hosting provider for account data.

What we do with your data

  • Provide the Raise tools and keep you signed in across them.
  • Show you your own data when you return.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with third parties except Supabase as described above, and Google Sheets if you choose to connect your own Google account.

Your choices

  • You can sign out at any time from any tool or from the Tools landing page.
  • You can disconnect Google Sheets at any time from within a tool. Disconnecting does not delete data already in your own spreadsheet.
  • To correct or delete your account data, contact us through the Contact link on raiseos.site.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Continued use of Raise after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reach us through the Contact link on raiseos.site.
