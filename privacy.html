Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026

Raise (raiseos.site) is a fundraising advisory brand for pre-seed and seed founders, run by Mouad Dliaa. This policy explains how personal data is handled on this site.

What this site collects

This site has no accounts, no sign-up, and no software tools. It does not collect personal data on its own. If you book a call, the booking is handled by Cal.com under its own policy. If you contact us, we use what you send only to reply.

What we do with your data

Anything you send us is used to respond to your inquiry and, if you become a client, to deliver the advisory work you agreed to. We do not sell personal data.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to ask us to delete it, contact us through the Contact link on raiseos.site.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Continued use of Raise after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy: reach us through the Contact link on raiseos.site.
