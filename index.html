WHY ROUNDS STALL

Why Great Startups Get Polite Passes.

Investors rarely pass because your product is bad. They pass because the investment story does not hold up. These are the six fatal narrative flaws—and how Raise solves each.

01. Unclear Investment Narrative

VCs cannot explain your moat in one simple sentence. If they cannot pitch it to their general partnership on Monday morning, they pass.

Raise answers with: Category positioning & 12-slide narrative overhaul.

02. Weak Deck Architecture

Burying the hook, cluttered appendices, confusing flow. How your slides are sequenced signals how clearly you operate as a founder.

Raise answers with: Slide-by-slide structure & cognitive load reduction.

03. Unsupported Market Claims

A top-down TAM built on wishful market reports collapses under the first partner question. Investors always verify the arithmetic.

Raise answers with: Bottom-up market sizing & verifiable unit economics.

04. Blind Spray-and-Pray Outreach

Spamming 500 junior associates burns months and your market reputation. Volume is not a fundraising strategy.

Raise answers with: 50 curated thesis-fit lead check writers & intro routing.

05. Unprepared for Tough Objections

"Why won't Big Tech copy this?" "Why now?" "What is your defensibility?" Hesitation in the first ten minutes ends the meeting.

Raise answers with: mock partner sessions & objection defense matrix.

06. Disorganized Meeting Cadence

Staggered chats spread across 6 months kill FOMO. Rounds die of momentum loss and investor hesitation, not direct rejection.

Raise answers with: 3-week parallel meeting sprint & weekly momentum updates.
