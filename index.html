Raise OS is a 5-phase fundraising system for pre-seed and seed founders: Readiness (score your deck before investors do), Aim (target 50 thesis-fit investors), Ignite (outreach and warm intros that convert), Sell (objection handling that wins partner meetings), and Execute (investor updates and FOMO sprints that close oversubscribed rounds). Every phase has a tool, a metric, and a playbook.

How much does pitch deck consulting cost?

Raise OS publishes three tiers: a £250 Deck Audit with 48-hour delivery, a £1,500 full Deck Rebuild Sprint over 14 days, and £3,500 end-to-end 1:1 Capital Raise Advisory over 30 days. The Deck Rater gives you a free Raise Score with no paywall, so you can diagnose your deck before spending anything.

What is a good Raise Score?

The Deck Rater scores your deck 0–100 across problem, solution, market, traction, team, and the ask. Below 60 means your deck is costing you meetings and needs a rebuild before any outreach. 60–79 is competitive but has fixable leaks. 80+ is investor-ready. Most decks we see for the first time score under 55.

How is this different from a pitch deck template or course?

Templates make you look like every other failed deck in a partner's inbox. Courses give you theory. Raise OS rebuilds your narrative around your unfair advantage, calibrated to your stage, round size, and geography, then installs the outreach and closing system around it. You get the thinking, not just the slides.

Is my deck kept 100% confidential under NDA?

Yes. We operate under a strict, automatic non-disclosure agreement. Your IP, metrics, and financials are never shared or published.

How fast will I receive my rebuilt deck?

Our Deck Audit Loom is delivered within 48 hours. Full Rebuild Sprints take 14 days from kickoff to final presentation deck.

What startup stages do you work with?

We specialize in Pre-Seed, Seed, and Series A rounds raising between £250k and £5M across B2B SaaS, AI, FinTech, and HealthTech.

Should I hire a pitch deck agency, take a course, or use Raise OS?

Agencies hand you slides but keep the thinking, so you can't defend the narrative in the partner meeting. Courses give you theory with no feedback on your deck. Raise OS rebuilds your narrative with you, calibrated to your stage and round, then installs the investor targeting and closing system around it. You keep the playbook and the ability to run it.

How long does it take to raise a pre-seed round?

A focused pre-seed raise takes 4–8 weeks from first investor meeting to term sheet, when the deck is ready and the target list is thesis-fit. Most founders take 3–6 months because they start outreach with an unready deck and a spray-and-pray list. Raise OS compresses this by scoring the deck first (Readiness), then running outreach as a campaign (Ignite through Execute).
