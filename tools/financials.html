Results

Net burn

—

Net burn = Monthly burn − Monthly revenue

Runway

—

Runway (months) = Cash on hand / Net burn. If net burn is zero or negative, runway is not the constraint.

Burn multiple

—

Burn multiple = Net burn / (New monthly revenue added × 12). Lower is better; below 1 means efficient growth.

LTV : CAC

—

LTV : CAC = LTV / CAC. Above 3 is generally considered healthy.

CAC payback

—

CAC payback (months) = CAC / (ARPU × Gross margin), where ARPU = Monthly revenue / Paying customers.
