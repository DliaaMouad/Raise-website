FUNDRAISING STRATEGY · ADVISORY

Run your raise like a system, not a lottery.

Fundraising Strategy is the advisory side of Raise: a structured fundraising process run with you, from the first diagnostic of your story to a signed term sheet. We fix the story, the targeting, and the objection defense in order, and we rehearse the meetings before you walk into them. Structure before outreach: four stages, Clarity, Structure, Precision, Conviction, delivered through the five-stage Raise OS.

Who this is for

This is for you if

  • You are a pre-seed or seed founder preparing a round in the coming weeks
  • You are raising solo, without a co-founder or a finance team to run the process
  • Your deck exists, but the investment story is not landing in the room
  • You want a partner-level second brain on narrative, targeting, and pitch preparation

This is not for you if

  • You have no round planned and no timeline to raise
  • You are looking for a deck-design-only service: prettier slides, same story
  • You want someone to raise for you while you stay out of the process

How it works: the Raise Operating System

The four stages are how the work reads. These five stages are how it runs. Fundraising is not a lottery. It is an engineered campaign, and every stage produces an asset you keep.

01

Diagnose

Week 1

Audit your deck, narrative, and unit economics against 10 YC dimensions and find the fatal leaks before partners do.

You keep: a slide-by-slide audit with exact line-by-line fixes.

02

Rebuild

Weeks 1 to 2

Re-engineer the narrative so every slide stands on its own and every metric claim holds under fire.

You keep: the rebuilt deck and your category and moat positioning.

03

Target

Week 2

Identify the 50 lead partners who actively write checks for your stage, sector, check size, and thesis.

You keep: the 50-partner target matrix.

04

Simulate

Week 3

Rehearse the 20 toughest partner objections with data-backed answers.

You keep: the objection defense matrix.

05

Execute

Weeks 3 to 6

Run parallel partner meeting sprints with pipeline tracking and weekly momentum updates.

You keep: a clean data room, a reviewed financial model and cap table, and your meeting pipeline tracker.
