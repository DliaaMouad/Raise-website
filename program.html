One system. Five phases.

Every founder in the program runs the same operating system, tuned to their round. No templates, no generic advice.

1

Readiness

We tear your deck apart like a partner would. Every narrative leak, weak proof point, and unanswered objection gets flagged before an investor ever sees it.

2

Aim

We build your target list from 754 verified VCs and accelerators. Right stage, right check size, right thesis. No spray and pray.

3

Ignite

Your story gets rebuilt around the one belief an investor must hold to write the check. Positioning that makes you the obvious pick, not one of fifty.

4

Sell

Outreach sequences, warm intro mapping, and pitch dry-runs. You walk into every meeting knowing the objections before they are raised.

5

Execute

Term sheet to close. We help you run a tight process, create competitive tension, and negotiate from strength until the money is wired.

What is inside the program

Everything is done with you, not handed to you as homework.

✓Slide-by-slide deck rebuild

Your full deck rewritten and redesigned around investor psychology. Every slide earns its place or it gets cut. The average rebuild removes a third of the slides and doubles the clarity.

✓Narrative restructuring

We find the sharpest version of your story: the villain, the inevitable shift, and why you win. Founders come in with features. They leave with a thesis.

✓Curated investor targeting

A ranked hit list from 754 verified pre-seed and seed VCs plus accelerators worldwide, matched to your stage, sector, and geography, with how-to-pitch notes on each.

✓Pitch dry-runs

Live practice sessions where we play the skeptical partner. You get grilled on traction, moat, and go-to-market until your answers are airtight.
